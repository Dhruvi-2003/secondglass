INDEPENDENT PORTFOLIO PILOT · INDIA

How it works / limits

A small lens-comparison project built around traceable observations, clear uncertainty and direct retailer links.

Where the data comes from

Prices are collected automatically from a fixed selection of public retailer product pages, using structured product data (JSON-LD) and targeted HTML extraction. Source selection, model mappings and policy reviews are done manually. Prices are not typed in by hand or generated by AI.

The site publishes a snapshot rather than contacting retailers while you browse. Exact model and mount matches can share a card; each store’s condition, reviews, warranty and returns remain separate. Missing facts stay unknown and conflicting claims are flagged. Coverage is selective, not every lens or retailer.

How fresh is it?

An hourly scheduled job attempts to refresh eligible sources. GitHub may delay a run; access blocks, page changes or failed checks can prevent an update. A listing’s Last checked date in Details shows its last successful source read in Indian Standard Time (IST), not the time the website was built.

Failed collectors retain older observations with their original dates. Observations older than 24 hours are marked stale. A recent check still does not guarantee current stock, delivery eligibility or the checkout price.

Source access and terms

Retailer terms and robots policies were reviewed on 1 November 2026. The review identified restrictions on automated collection or public reuse at several sites, and one source’s terms could not be verified. All collectors currently remain enabled at the project owner’s request. A permissive robots file is not permission or a licence.

This is a manual review, not an automated policy-enforcement system. We have not obtained written retailer permissions and do not claim otherwise. The collector does not bypass logins or access challenges. See the source-by-source review findings.

What we chose not to build

This GitHub Pages version has no checkout, payments, accounts, email alerts, live delivery checks or AI chatbot. It does not collect in-app usage or feedback; GitHub may retain hosting logs. Your shortlist stays in your browser. Price history is limited to the published observation.

We do not inspect individual lenses, certify sellers, verify every warranty claim or combine reviews across stores. Classifieds, private sellers, rentals and enquiry-only listings are excluded. Avoiding these features keeps the pilot small and its limitations visible.

Before you buy

Confirm current price, stock, shipping, compatibility, warranty and return terms with the seller. Marketplace merchants, coupons and delivery eligibility can change. Retailer names, logos and links identify sources; they imply no affiliation, endorsement, partnership or seller approval.
